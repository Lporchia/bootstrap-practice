A Quiet Trail Is Becoming a Popular Place to Visit

By Alex Morgan

Beautiful mountain scenery. Photo by Tobi from Pixels.

Just outside the fictional town of Pine Hollow, a mountain trail has become a favorite place for neighbors to spend time outdoors.

The change started with a sign at the trailhead inviting visitors to take a walk and enjoy the view. Soon, neighbors began meeting there on Saturday mornings.

“You don’t need to go far to find somewhere new,” said fictional resident Jamie Lee. Lee enjoys the peaceful views and the chance to listen to the wind.

Red moon. Photo by Pedro Figueras.

Some residents now volunteer to clean up the trail and remind visitors to stay on the path. They hope to keep the area clean and welcoming.

“The point isn’t to make the trail bigger. It’s to notice what’s already here.”

Visitors can follow the mountain trail and help keep it clean by taking their rubbish with them.
